Sunrise Dance Studio Renovation

Transforming the barn loft into a light-filled movement sanctuary for community jams, family classes, and daytime celebrations.

Project Intentions

The Sunrise Dance Studio will anchor our village gatherings with a dedicated movement space. We are opening the barn loft, refreshing the floors, updating lighting, and installing sound and acoustic treatments that support sober daytime dance parties, family classes, and intimate performance salons.
